# Organic content research

Most videos about a hot topic flop. This skill finds what already worked in your niche, so you copy the pattern, not the video.

The whole method is two columns:

- **Topic:** what the video is about. The information or the situation.
- **Format:** how it is shown. Skit, meme edit, voiceover over footage, side-by-side, silent animation and so on.

A video wins when a good topic meets a good format. When you can see both columns, you can take any topic and put it in a format that already won.

## Your settings

Fill these in, or say them when you run the skill. If the niche is blank, ask for it before anything else.

- Niche: [YOUR NICHE]
- Platform: [YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels or TikTok]
- Window: the last 30 days
- Creators to check: 10 to 20
- Outlier bar: 10x the creator's normal views

## Step 1. Find the top creators

Search the platform for the niche. Collect the channels that post short videos about it again and again, not one-off posters.

Done when you have 10 to 20 creators who post about the niche every week.

## Step 2. Find each creator's normal

For each creator, take their last 20 to 30 short videos. Their **normal** is the median view count of those videos (the middle number, so one viral video does not move it).

Done when every creator has a normal. Skip a creator with fewer than 10 videos: their normal is a guess.

## Step 3. Find the outliers

For every video, work out **x median** = views divided by that creator's normal.

- **10x or more:** outlier. The idea won, not the audience.
- **2x to 10x:** above normal.
- **Under 1x:** below normal or a flop.

Compare videos of a similar age. A video posted yesterday has not finished growing.

Done when you have a list of outliers with their views, normal and x median.

## Step 4. Split every video into topic and format

For each outlier, write one line for the topic and one or two words for the format. Use the same format names every time, so they can be counted.

Then count: which formats show up most among the outliers? Which formats only show up in flops?

Keep a few flops in the table too. A topic that won in one format and flopped in another tells you the format was the reason.

Done when you have a table with these columns:

| Video | Topic | Format | Result | x median | Views | Creator | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Step 5. Put a winning format around your idea

Take your own idea, in your own niche, and the top formats from step 4. Write 3 versions of your idea, one per format. For each one, give:

1. The first frame and the first line someone hears or reads.
2. What happens on screen, step by step.
3. Why this format fits this idea, in one line.

Done when you have 3 versions you could film today.

## Rules

- Only real numbers. Every view count comes from the platform's own page. If you cannot see a number, say so. Never guess one.
- Link every video, so anyone can check it.
- Say the date you pulled the numbers. Views keep growing.
- Copy the pattern, never the video. Use your own footage, your own words and your own idea.
