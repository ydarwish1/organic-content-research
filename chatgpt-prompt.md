# The ChatGPT prompt

No Claude? Paste this into ChatGPT (with web search on). Change the niche in the first line.

```text
My niche is GTA 6.

1. Find the top 10 to 20 creators who post short videos about it every week (YouTube Shorts, Reels or TikTok).
2. For each creator, find their normal: the median views of their last 20 to 30 short videos.
3. Show me which videos got 10x or more of that creator's normal views. Use only real view counts from the platform. Link every video.
4. Split every one of those videos into two columns: the topic (what it's about) and the format (how it's shown, like a skit, meme edit or voiceover over gameplay).
5. Put it all in one table: Video, Topic, Format, x median, Views, Creator, Link. Then tell me which formats won most often.
```

Then, for your own video:

```text
Put this format around my niche idea: [the format] + [your idea].
Give me the first frame, the first line, and what happens on screen.
```
