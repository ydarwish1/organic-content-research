# Organic content research

Everybody is about to post about GTA 6, and most of it will flop. Here are the two things from my video:

## 1. The GTA 6 Shorts Breakdown

👉 **[gta6-shorts-breakdown.pdf](gta6-shorts-breakdown.pdf)** (the PDF) or **[gta6-shorts-breakdown.md](gta6-shorts-breakdown.md)** (the same list as a page)

37 GTA 6 Shorts, split into **topic** (what it's about) and **format** (how it's shown), with how far each one beat its creator's normal views. Picked from 3,086 GTA 6 Shorts across 121 channels. Also as a spreadsheet: [gta6-shorts-breakdown.csv](gta6-shorts-breakdown.csv).

## 2. The AI skill that does the content research for you

👉 **[skill/organic-content-research/SKILL.md](skill/organic-content-research/SKILL.md)**

It finds the top creators in your niche, shows you which videos got 10x their normal views, splits each one into topic and format, then puts a winning format around your own idea.

**Use it in Claude Code:** copy the `skill/organic-content-research` folder into `~/.claude/skills/`. Then say "content research for GTA 6" (or your own niche).

**Use it in ChatGPT:** paste the prompt from [chatgpt-prompt.md](chatgpt-prompt.md).

## The idea in one line

Take any topic, and put it in a format that already won.
