# Organic content research

Find what already won in your niche. Then put a winning format around your own idea.

It is a fill in the blank: you write your niche, and the AI does the research.

## 1. The guide (PDF)

👉 **[organic-content-research.pdf](organic-content-research.pdf)**

The method in 5 steps, and the prompt you can copy.

## 2. The AI skill that does the content research for you

👉 **[skill/organic-content-research/SKILL.md](skill/organic-content-research/SKILL.md)**

It finds the top creators in your niche, shows you which videos got 10x their normal views, splits each one into topic and format, then puts a winning format around your own idea.

**Use it in Claude Code:** copy the `skill/organic-content-research` folder into `~/.claude/skills/`. Then say "content research for [your niche]".

**Use it in ChatGPT:** paste the prompt from [chatgpt-prompt.md](chatgpt-prompt.md).

## The idea in one line

Take any topic, and put it in a format that already won.
